Reading movement

Behaviour, routine, gait

Smart collar for canine health

A collar-mounted IMU with semi-automatic video labelling and segmentation of 20 dog behaviours, including eating, drinking and urination, turned into daily activity reports. Includes a pedometer and gait features toward detecting patellar luxation.

M.S. thesis · 2023–2025

Full-body motion from fewer IMUs

Reconstructing full-body pose from 4–5 body-worn IMUs instead of the usual 6, with transformer models trained on AMASS, DIP-IMU and in-house recordings.

Explaining every band.

My Ph.D. work on biological spectra.

An accurate classifier can still be reading the substrate instead of the sample. Given a short sample description and one spectrum, my method assigns each band to a molecule, a vibration and its environment. A spectrum counts as explained when what is left over matches replicate noise.

y(ν) = Σₖ aₖ · L(ν; ν̃ₖ(env), γₖ) + B(ν) + ε(ν)
