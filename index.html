Our bodies know things before we do. I use computer science to read their signals and bring that knowledge back to people.

I started with wearables that read how we move and how our heart responds. I now work with Raman and SERS spectroscopy, which reads the molecules a body carries, and build methods that explain every band of a biological spectrum by the molecule behind it. I also co-founded rAI, which makes Raman analysis usable without a spectroscopist.

Ph.D. work

Explaining biological spectra, band by band.

An accurate classifier on SERS data can still be reading the substrate instead of the sample. My Ph.D. builds a method that, given a short sample description and one measured spectrum, assigns each band to a molecule, a vibration and its chemical environment. A spectrum counts as explained when what is left over matches replicate noise.

y(ν) = Σₖ aₖ · L(ν; ν̃ₖ(env), γₖ) + B(ν) + ε(ν), ε ~ replicate noise

Peptides

20 amino acids and 2 dipeptides, about 200 spectra each. Every band of a known biomolecule, explained.
